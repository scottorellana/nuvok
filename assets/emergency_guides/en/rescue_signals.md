# Rescue Signals

## SOS (universally recognized)

### Morse
- **S**: 3 short pits
- **O**: 3 long pits
- **S**: 3 short pits

### Visual
- 3 fires in triangle
- 3 light flashes
- 3 stacked rocks + fire

## Fire Signals

### Geographic position
- On beach: write in sand
- On snow: stomp/rocks
- On mountain: rocks in line

### Colors
- Orange fire = I need help
- White fire = I need supplies
- Green fire = I'm OK

## Air Signals

### Solar panel (V-SIG)
- **V** on ground = I need medical assistance
- **X** on ground = I need help
- **I** on ground = I need information
- **F** on ground = I need fuel

### Size
- Minimum 3m long
- 30cm wide
- Contrastive (rocks vs sand)

## Whistle

### Code
- 1 blast = I'm here
- 2 blasts = I need help
- 3 blasts = medical emergency
- Long = OK/confirmed

### Technique
- 3 cycles, then wait 1 min
- Repeat until heard

## Satellite Phone

### Position
- Line of sight to sky
- Antenna elevated if possible
- Avoid metallic structures

### Short messages
- Exact lat/long
- Number of people
- Type of emergency
- Supplies available

## Emergency Beacon (PLB)

### Activation
1. Extend antenna
2. Press ON
3. Hold until green LED
4. DO NOT turn off until rescued

### Position
- As high as possible
- Clear of water if possible
- Antenna pointing up
