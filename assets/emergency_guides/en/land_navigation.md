# Land Navigation

## Using the sun

### Sunrise/sunset
- Sun rises in EAST
- Sun sets in WEST

### Noon
- Sun indicates SOUTH (northern hemisphere)
- Sun indicates NORTH (southern hemisphere)

## Using stars

### Southern Cross (southern hemisphere)
- 4 stars in cross
- Long arm points to SOUTH

### Big Dipper (northern hemisphere)
- 7 stars in ladle
- The 2 front stars point to Polaris
- Polaris = NORTH

### Full moon
- Rises in EAST at 6pm
- Sets in WEST at 6am

## Homemade compass

### Needle and magnet
1. Rub needle with magnet 50 times
2. Place needle on cork
3. Float in water, points north

### Analog watch
1. Point hour hand at sun
2. Bisector = SOUTH (NH) / NORTH (SH)

## Map reading

### Scale
- 1:50,000 = 1cm = 500m
- 1:25,000 = 1cm = 250m

### Contour lines
- Close together = steep slope
- Far apart = flat terrain
- V-shape = valley
- M-shape = mountain

### Orientation
- North always up
- Wind rose = north

## Offline GPS

### Download maps before
- Maps.me or OsmAnd
- Offline = works without signal

### Coordinates
- Format: DD°MM.mmm' (standard GPS)
- Always carry paper with coordinates
