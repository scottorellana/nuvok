# Emergency Garden

The pantry runs out in weeks; **the garden starts paying off in 20-30 days** if you plant the right thing today. This is not gardening: it is calorie logistics.

## Critical steps

1. **Plant the fast crops TODAY:** radish (20-30 days), leafy greens — chard, spinach, mustard greens, cut-and-come-again lettuce (30-45), beans/green beans (50-60), cucumber and zucchini (45-60). The pantry potato that sprouted = seed (pieces with an "eye", 60-90 days).
2. **Seeds without a packet:** from the market/pantry can work — beans, lentils, unprocessed corn, tomato (ferment the seeds 2 days and dry), chili pepper, papaya, ayote. Toasted/polished items do NOT germinate.
3. **Staggered planting:** every 2 weeks plant another batch of the same crop — continuous harvest instead of a binge and then hunger.
4. **Sun + water determines the location:** 6+ hours of sun, near reuse water (graywater cascade WITHOUT strong soap), and in any container: tires, sacks, broken buckets, sheet-metal troughs.
5. **Fast living soil:** soil + compost/old manure + rotted leaves. Camp compost (peels, cold ash) feeds the cycle — nothing organic is thrown away.
6. **Protect the investment:** simple fence (branches, mesh, sacks) against chickens and dogs, and water at dawn/dusk directly at the root (midday evaporates and burns).
7. **Harvest leaves WITHOUT killing the plant:** cut outer leaves of chard/mustard greens and leave the center — the same plant produces for months. Save the best seeds from what you harvest: garden 2 starts for free.

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|----------|------------|
| Planting only corn "because it fills you up" | Fast crops first (radish/greens); corn takes 90-120 days |
| Everything at once | Staggered every 2 weeks |
| Watering at midday with a stream of water | Dawn/dusk at the root |
| Graywater with soap/chlorine on the garden | Only rinse water or wash water without strong chemicals |
| Pulling up the whole plant for a few leaves | Outer leaves: the plant keeps producing |
| Throwing away peels and ash | Compost: it is the fertilizer for month 2 |

## Calories per meter (space priority)

1. Potato/sweet potato: kings of calories per m².
2. Beans/green beans: protein + fixes nitrogen (improves the soil).
3. Squash/ayote: yields well, stores for months.
4. Leafy greens: fast vitamins that prevent disease.

## Example: Emergency Garden

**Situation:** Week 2 after the disaster; donated food will last a month and there is a sunny vacant lot next to the camp.

**Do:** 1. Today: clear 5×5 m and plant radish + mustard greens + whatever sprouts from the pantry (potato, beans). 2. Market tomatoes: ferment seeds and start a seedbed in cans. 3. Branch fence and watering shifts with rinse water. 4. In 2 weeks: second batch and transplant seedlings. 5. Communal compost in the corner of the lot.

**Avoid:** Do not wait for "the good seeds" to start, do not plant the whole lot with corn, do not water with soapy water, do not leave the lot without a fence.

**Escalate:** If the donation is cut off before the harvest: prioritize calories (potato/beans/ayote) in every free meter, activate fishing/traps if there is a river (guide), and coordinate harvest exchanges with the neighborhood over the mesh — garden diversity is everyone’s insurance.
