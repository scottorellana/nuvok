# Safe River Water

The river gives endless water — **and endless ways to get sick**. Turbidity, parasites and whatever lies upstream decide whether it saves you or knocks you down.

## Critical steps

1. **Think UPSTREAM.** Towns, livestock, crops, dead animals? The water tells the story from above. Take from fast, stony stretches, never from rotten still pools.
2. **Take from the clean side.** Moving water over stones > still bank. Submerge the container at mid-depth: the surface carries what floats, the bottom carries sediment.
3. **Settle murky water FIRST.** Muddy water: let it rest 1-2 hours (or overnight) and use only the top ⅔. Chlorine and boiling work poorly with mud.
4. **Filter in layers.** Cut bottle upside down: cloth, ground charcoal from the fire, fine sand, gravel. Filtering removes the visible — it does NOT disinfect.
5. **ALWAYS disinfect after filtering.** Boil 1 minute (3 min at altitude) OR 2 drops of 5% chlorine per liter + 30 min (settled murky water: double dose, see the calculator).
6. **Sun as plan C (SODIS).** Full transparent PET bottle, 6 hours in direct sun on a reflective surface: UV disinfects. CLEAR water only.
7. **Protect the clean container.** Purifying and refilling with the same dirty cup ruins everything: separate "raw" from "potable" and rinse the thread with treated water.

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|----------|------------|
| Drinking straight "because it runs and looks clean" | Running does not kill giardia or cholera: always disinfect |
| Chlorinating muddy water directly | Settle/filter first; mud shields microbes |
| Taking from the still pool by the bank | Fast, stony stretch, at mid-depth |
| Filtering and calling it safe | A homemade filter does NOT disinfect: boil or chlorinate after |
| Potable container refilled with the raw cup | Separate raw/potable religiously |
| Ignoring the group's diarrhea "because of the rush" | Oral rehydration NOW (the calculator) — it dehydrates more than thirst |

## The river water ladder

1. **Best:** a small, fast tributary running clean into the river.
2. **Good:** the river at a fast stretch, settled + boiled.
3. **Fair:** murky water settled + filtered + double chlorine.
4. **Last:** a bank well — dig 2-5 m from the river: the sand pre-filters (disinfect anyway).

## Example: River Water

**Situation:** Camp beside a chocolate-colored, swollen river; the bottles ran out and there are children in the group.

**Do:** 1. Dig a bank well 3 m from the water and let it fill filtered through the sand. 2. Meanwhile: settle 2 pots of water from the fast stretch. 3. Build the bottle filter with charcoal from the fire. 4. Boil EVERYTHING 1 minute or double chlorine if there is no fire. 5. Label containers: raw vs potable.

**Avoid:** Do not let the children drink "just a sip" untreated, do not chlorinate mud directly, do not refill the potable container with the raw cup, do not take from the still pool.

**Escalate:** Diarrhea or vomiting in the group: oral rehydration NOW with the proportions from the calculator and switch to the poisoning guide; if there is blood in the diarrhea or high fever, it is an evacuation priority — trigger the mesh SOS.
