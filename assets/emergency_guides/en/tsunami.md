# Tsunami

With a tsunami there is only one move: **HIGH GROUND, on foot, now**. It is not a wave: it is the entire sea pushing for minutes, and the first one is usually not the worst.

## Critical steps

1. **The earthquake IS the alarm.** On the coast, a strong or long earthquake (20+ seconds) = evacuate to high ground WITHOUT waiting for sirens or confirmation. Minutes of doubt make the difference.
2. **Natural signs = run:** the sea recedes, leaving the seabed dry; a roar like an airplane/train from the sea; strange water behavior. Any one of the three: DO NOT look — go up.
3. **ON FOOT and UPHILL:** 30+ meters of elevation or 3+ km inland. The car gets stuck in traffic with everyone fleeing: the person who climbs on foot up the slope arrives; the person in the car in line does not.
4. **No natural high ground: tall concrete building** (4+ floors, rooftop) is your urban plan B. Trees only as a last resort.
5. **STAY up for 12 hours or until official notice.** A tsunami is a train of waves separated by 10-60 min; the second or third is usually larger. “It’s over” kills those who go down to look.
6. **Trapped by the water: hold on to something large that floats** (roof, log) and DO NOT fight the outgoing current — it carries you out to sea and back; conserve energy, protect yourself from debris (the water is a blender of cars and walls).
7. **Afterward:** the water leaves traps — leaks, wires, undermined structures, and victims who need the CPR/wounds guides. The mesh reunites the family (HIGH meeting point agreed on beforehand).

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|----------|------------|
| Go down to the beach to see the receded sea | It is the ultimate sign: run uphill |
| Evacuate by car along the coast | On foot, perpendicular to the sea, uphill |
| Wait for the siren after the strong earthquake | The earthquake IS the order on the coast |
| Return after the first wave | 12 h up high or official notice: more are coming |
| Take shelter on a “solid” ground floor | Height: floor 4+ or hillside |
| Swim against the outgoing current | Float on something large and protect yourself from debris |

## Prepare your coast

- Know YOUR vertical route: which hillside/building within 15 min on foot?
- HIGH family meeting point on the mesh (not on the beach).
- Drill with children: “strong shaking = we go up without Dad if necessary; he goes up his way.”

## Example: Tsunami

**Situation:** Coastal town; a long earthquake shakes, and when you look out, the sea begins to recede, leaving boats stranded.

**Do:** 1. Shout “TSUNAMI, UPHILL!” as you leave — your voice saves neighbors. 2. Family on foot by the agreed vertical route, each person goes up: the meeting point is UPHILL, not the house. 3. Help whoever you can get up without stopping. 4. Up high: headcount by mesh, no one goes down. 5. 12 hours minimum or official notice.

**Avoid:** Do not carry things, do not get the car, do not go down “to help” between waves, do not trust the calm of the interval.

**Escalate:** If someone was swept away and appears: CPR if they do not respond (CPR guide), treat wounds and hypothermia; report it on the mesh with position so the group can direct the rescue — and no one else goes down until the notice.
