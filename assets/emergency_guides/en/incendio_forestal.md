# Wildfire

A wildfire runs **uphill faster than you do**, shifts with the wind and kills more by smoke and panic than by flame.

## Critical steps

1. **Evacuate EARLY or do not evacuate.** The worst decision is the late one: cars trapped in smoke on an exit road. Visible smoke approaching + a warning = leave NOW with the ICE card and the kit.
2. **Flee SIDEWAYS and DOWNHILL from the fire,** never up the slope nor in its own direction with the wind favoring the fire (it doubles its speed uphill).
3. **Head for what has already burned or does not burn:** cold black zones, rocks, lakes/rivers, plowed fields, wide roads. A large burned area is the safest refuge in the forest.
4. **Trapped with no way out:** low clear ground (ditch, road cut), face down, feet TOWARD the fire, covered with earth/a non-synthetic blanket, breathing the air right at the ground through wet cloth. The front passes in minutes: hold on.
5. **Trapped in a vehicle:** do NOT get out and run between the flames — windows closed, engine off, lights on, body below the dashboard with a blanket: the car shields you from the front's radiant heat even if it gets damaged.
6. **Smoke kills first:** wet cloth over nose/mouth, move low, and NEVER into dense smoke even if "the way out is through there" — with no visibility there is no way out.
7. **Defend the house ONLY with margin:** close everything, remove flammable material to 10 m, fill tubs/buckets, cotton clothing — and even then, if the authorities say leave: LEAVE.

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|----------|------------|
| "Waiting to see" as the smoke grows | Evacuate early by wide routes |
| Fleeing up the slope | Sideways and down, to burned/clear ground |
| Running between the flames from the car | Inside, under the dashboard, windows closed |
| Synthetic clothes to evacuate | Cotton/wool: synthetics melt into the skin |
| Going back for belongings/animals into the front | Nothing is worth the fire front |
| Wetting your clothes and walking through flames | Steam scalds: wet only the cloth you breathe through |

## Preparation in a risk zone

- 10 m clear around the house (dry leaves, firewood far away).
- Kit and documents ready in the dry season + two agreed routes (the mesh meeting point).
- The mesh tactical map shares fronts and blocked routes with the neighborhood.

## Example: Wildfire

**Situation:** A smoke column 2 km away growing with the wind toward your village; the only main exit passes near the front.

**Do:** 1. IMMEDIATE departure: family + ICE + water; post the front on the mesh map. 2. Lateral route moving away from the wind's axis, even if it is longer. 3. If smoke closes the route: turn around to the burned zone/open field, not "let's see if we get through". 4. Wet cloth to breathe, lights on. 5. Regroup at the meeting point and report who is missing.

**Avoid:** Do not wait "for another warning", do not climb the hill to look, do not drive into dense smoke, do not go back for the livestock with the front upon you.

**Escalate:** If the fire catches you en route: closed vehicle under the dashboard, or a clear ditch feet-to-the-fire covered with earth; after the front passes, move to the cold burned ground and treat burns and inhalation (burns and CPR guides) with the mesh SOS.
