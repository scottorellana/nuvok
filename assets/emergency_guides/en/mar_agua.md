# Drinking Water at Sea and on the Coast

Surrounded by water you can NOT drink: **seawater dehydrates you twice as fast as thirst**. Your life depends on rain, condensation, and knowing where to look on the coast.

## Critical steps

1. **NEVER drink seawater.** Not "a little," not mixed: your kidneys spend more water flushing out the salt than you take in. It speeds up death and clouds your judgment first.
2. **Set up rain capture NOW.** Spread out every cloth/tarp/plastic sheet BEFORE it rains, center sagging into a container. Rinse off the built-up salt with the first rain, then collect.
3. **Dawn dew.** On a boat or the coast: wipe every smooth surface (fiberglass, sails, large leaves) with cloth before the sun comes up, and wring it out.
4. **On the coast: dig behind the first dune.** 30-60 m from the sea, dig until water appears; the FRESH rain layer floats on top of the salty one — take only the surface with a cloth or cup, without stirring up the hole.
5. **Look for stream mouths and rocks.** Streams running down to the sea, cliff cracks that drip, rain pools in HIGH rocks (low ones are salt spray: test with your lip, do not swallow).
6. **Green coconuts = safe water** on tropical coasts: the green fallen ones at the foot of the palm. Very ripe (brown) ones give less water and more oil: a laxative in excess.
7. **Distill if you have fire or sun.** Boil seawater under a cloth that traps the steam and wring it out (crude but it works), or build a solar still with a container of salt water inside.

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|----------|------------|
| Drinking seawater "to trick your thirst" | NEVER: you lose more water than you gain |
| Drinking urine or fish blood | They dehydrate you just the same; fish eye/spine fluid in extreme emergency is minimal |
| Waiting for rain to rig the capture | Everything ready BEFORE; tropical rain lasts minutes |
| Drinking from the bottom of the beach well | Only the fresh surface layer, without stirring |
| Low pools next to the surf | They are salt spray; only high rain pools |
| Eating a lot without water (dried fish, ripe coconut) | Digesting protein/fat SPENDS water |

## Castaway priorities

1. Flotation and signals first (you get rescued before you die of thirst).
2. Rain capture rigged at all times.
3. Ration SWEAT: shade with the sail/tarp, wet your CLOTHES with seawater by day to cool off (they dry before nightfall), stay still during the hot hours.
4. Do not eat if you have no water to go with the food.

## Example: Water at Sea

**Situation:** Adrift in a boat with no engine since yesterday; half a liter left and the sky threatens distant rain.

**Do:** 1. Rig the tarp NOW as a funnel into the bucket, and tie extra cloths to the edges. 2. Drink your half liter as real thirst demands, do not "save" it. 3. At dawn wipe the dew off the whole boat and wring it out. 4. Wet your shirt with seawater during the hot hours and stay in the shade of the sail. 5. If it rains: rinse for 30 seconds, then collect EVERYTHING.

**Avoid:** Do not drink a single drop of seawater, do not eat the dried fish without water, do not leave the capture unrigged, do not sit in the sun.

**Escalate:** If someone drank seawater and becomes confused or has a seizure, treat it as a heat stroke/seizure emergency and prioritize ALL the fresh water for them; when you reach the coast, dig behind the dune before walking inland.
