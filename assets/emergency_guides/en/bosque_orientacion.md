# Not Getting Lost in the Forest

The golden rule the moment you feel lost: **STOP** — **S**top, **T**hink, **O**bserve, **P**lan. Lost people who walk in panic move away from rescue; those who stop, survive.

## Critical steps

1. **STOP the instant you doubt.** Sit down, drink water, take 10 breaths. The first hour of panic produces the 10 km that doom you.
2. **Think: what is the last CERTAIN place you remember?** The junction, the river, the big rock. How long ago? That's your maximum distance from the trail.
3. **Observe your resources.** Battery, water, warmth, remaining daylight. Under 3 hours of light: build shelter NOW, navigation is for tomorrow.
4. **Plan: staying put almost always wins.** If anyone knows your route or you triggered the mesh/SOS, stay: a fixed target is found 10 times faster than a moving one.
5. **If you do move, move straight.** In forest you walk in circles without references: ALWAYS line up 2 trees ahead; on reaching the first, line up a third. Downhill running water usually leads to people.
6. **Mark your trail.** Every 20-30 m: broken branches pointing your direction, stone arrows, cloth strips. It helps rescuers AND lets you backtrack.
7. **Be visible and audible.** 3 of anything = international distress: 3 whistle blasts (whistle is in Tools), 3 fires, 3 flashes. Repeat every few minutes.

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|-----------|-----------|
| Walking "a bit further" once in doubt | STOP immediately: every doubtful step moves you from rescue |
| Walking at night | Shelter at dusk; night manufactures sprained ankles |
| Cutting cross-country to "make up time" | Backtrack along your own marked trail |
| Draining the battery on constant signal attempts | Power the phone in windows: high points, once an hour |
| Following water blindly through canyons | Water guides, but go around waterfalls and gorges |
| Splitting the group to "find the way" | The group NEVER splits |

## Orientation without a compass (the Navigation guide expands)

- **Sun:** rises east, sets west; at midday it marks south (northern hemisphere).
- **Shadow stick:** plant a stick, mark the shadow tip, wait 15 min, mark again: the line between marks runs west→east.
- **The app's compass** (Tools) works with no signal.
- Set a bearing and TRUST it: the feeling of "it's that way" is the enemy.

## Example: Not Getting Lost in the Forest

**Situation:** You left the trail for some fruit and when you turned around nothing looks familiar; 4 hours of light left.

**Do:** 1. Sit down and take 10 breaths. 2. Reconstruct: the trail was uphill, ~15 min ago. 3. Mark your current spot with a big X of branches. 4. Walk straight uphill lining up trees, marking every 20 m. 5. If the trail hasn't appeared in 30 min, backtrack to your X along your marks and set up shelter and signals.

**Avoid:** Don't wander in circles "exploring", don't drop into the gully because it's easier, don't drain the battery, don't keep walking at dusk.

**Escalate:** With injury, nightfall or closing weather: stop navigating — shelter + 3 signals + mesh SOS; if cell coverage appears even briefly, call the emergency number (CALL button).
