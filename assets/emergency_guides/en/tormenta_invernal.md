# Winter Storm

The winter storm kills after the snowfall: **in the cold blackout, on the icy road and with the generator inside the house**. You win by staying put and warm.

## Critical steps

1. **Stay where you are.** 70% of deaths happen in vehicles and out in the open. With a storm warning: nobody goes out, full tank, water and food for 3 days, batteries charged.
2. **Cold blackout: ONE room.** The whole family into one small sun-facing room, doors closed, gaps sealed with towels, mattresses on the floor, layers and shared blankets. You heat people, not the house.
3. **CO: the blackout's killer.** Generator/grill/brazier NEVER inside nor in the garage (not even with the door open) — 6+ m outside, away from windows. Headache + nausea + drowsiness in several people = CO: everyone OUTSIDE now.
4. **Protect the water:** a minimal drip from one tap (still pipes burst), and fill the tub BEFORE the storm (reserve + toilet).
5. **Trapped in the car:** stay INSIDE (the blizzard disorients within meters of the car). Engine 10 min per hour for heat WITH the exhaust clear of snow (CO), leeward window barely open, antenna/cloth up high as a signal, and move hands/feet.
6. **Dress to survive indoors:** loose layers, hat ALWAYS (even sleeping), spare dry socks. Eat and drink warm things regularly: calories are heating (extreme cold guide).
7. **Ice on cables and branches:** after the freeze, downed cables loaded with ice and branches that give way — 10 m of distance, and no walking under loaded trees.

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|----------|------------|
| "I can make it before it gets worse" | Stay: the blizzard closes in within minutes |
| Generator in the "ventilated" garage | 6+ m outside, away from windows |
| Heating the whole house with the oven open | One small room and people together; the oven never as heating |
| Leaving the trapped car to "get help" | Inside, signal up high, engine 10 min/h with a clear exhaust |
| Sleeping without a hat "because it is uncomfortable" | The head loses disproportionate heat |
| Walking under ice-loaded trees/cables | Open routes, 10 m from the cables |

## Storm kit (before the season)

- House: water for 3 days, no-cook food, extra blankets, batteries, radio.
- Car: blanket, bottle, snack bar, small shovel, sand/mat (traction), charger.
- Neighbors: who has a fireplace/wood stove? The neighborhood network spreads the heat (community organization).

## Example: Winter Storm

**Situation:** Blackout in the middle of the storm; the house is losing heat and 3 days of snow are forecast.

**Do:** 1. Everyone to the small room on the sunny side: mattresses, blankets, towels in the gaps. 2. Tap on a drip and tub filled if there is still pressure. 3. Cook on the grill OUTSIDE and only the pot comes in. 4. Hats on, regular warm food, shifts to watch children and the elderly (the first to get cold). 5. Neighborhood status over the mesh: who needs shelter with the fireplace family?

**Avoid:** Do not light the brazier inside "for a little while", do not go out to clear the roof in the middle of the blizzard, do not leave an elderly person sleeping alone in a cold room, do not use candles near blankets.

**Escalate:** Group headache+dizziness = CO: everyone outside and ventilate before returning; uncontrollable shivering or confusion in someone = hypothermia (guide) — body heat and warm sugar; if the house stays below 0 °C sustained, move as a group to the house with firewood BEFORE nightfall.
