# Desert Hazards

What kills in the desert is rarely what the movies show: it's **the flash flood, the ground where you put your hand, and the wash where you make your bed**.

## Critical steps

1. **NEVER camp in a dry wash.** The flash flood is the desert's silent killer: rain 30 km away that you never see can send a wall of water at night. Always sleep on HIGH ground, even if the wash looks like the most comfortable, flattest spot.
2. **If the wash starts to roar or smells like wet earth, run uphill.** Don't cross moving water in an arroyo: it goes from puddle to torrent in minutes.
3. **Look before you touch.** Snakes and scorpions rest in the shade you want: under rocks, wood, crevices. Roll rocks and wood TOWARD you (the animal escapes the other way) and never put your hand where you can't see.
4. **Shake out boots and clothes every morning.** Scorpions seek your warmth at night. Boot upside down + tap on the heel before putting it on: mandatory ritual.
5. **Walk with a stick and stomp.** Vibration warns snakes; most bites happen by stepping or reaching. If you see one: freeze, then back away slowly — never handle it, even "dead" (the head bites by reflex).
6. **Sandstorm: stop, cover, wait.** Mark your heading (stone arrow) BEFORE it hits, cover nose and mouth with cloth, protect your eyes, back to the wind against a windbreak, and wait: walking in a sandstorm means getting lost.
7. **Respect the night cold.** After a 40 °C day, pre-dawn can hit 0 °C: desert hypothermia is real (heat and shelter guide).

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|-----------|-----------|
| Camping in the dry wash "so flat and comfy" | ALWAYS high ground, away from the channel |
| Reaching into crevices, holes or under rocks | Look first, roll rocks toward you |
| Putting boots on without shaking them | Upside down and heel tap, every time |
| Handling a "dead" snake | The head bites by reflex hours later |
| Walking through a sandstorm | Stop, mark heading, cover up and wait |
| Cutting/sucking a snakebite | Immobilize, note the time, go to the bites guide |

## If a snake bites

1. Get away from the snake; sit down and stay CALM (a racing heart spreads venom).
2. Remove rings/watch/boots from the limb NOW (it will swell).
3. Immobilize the limb at heart level or below; write down the time and mark the swelling edge with a pen.
4. NO tourniquet, cutting, sucking or ice.
5. Walk the MINIMUM; be carried if possible → bites and stings guide.

## Scorpion sting

- Intense local pain: wash, cold in cloth, painkiller from your kit.
- Watch 4 h: drooling, double vision, muscle spasms or trouble breathing = antivenom urgently, trigger SOS.
- Small children are ALWAYS an emergency with scorpions.

## Example: Desert Hazards

**Situation:** Dusk in arid terrain; the only flat, wind-sheltered ground is a dry wash of soft sand.

**Do:** 1. Reject it: climb 3-5 m to the nearest high bench even if less comfortable. 2. Before setting camp, check surrounding rocks and crevices by rolling them toward you. 3. Boots upside down inside the shelter. 4. Mark your heading with a stone arrow in case dawn brings a storm. 5. Prepare warm layers: pre-dawn will be cold.

**Avoid:** Don't sleep in the wash however comfortable, don't leave boots outside, don't reach under rocks in the dark, don't walk if a sandstorm arrives.

**Escalate:** If a flash flood still catches you: drop everything and climb to the HIGHEST point (don't cross the water); if a bite swells fast or breathing gets hard, trigger the mesh SOS and follow the bites guide — it's a race against the venom.
