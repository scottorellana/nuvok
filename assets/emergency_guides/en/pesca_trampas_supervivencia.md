# Emergency Food: Fishing and Traps

**Perspective first:** you survive WEEKS without food, but only days without water and hours without shelter. Food is priority 4 — don't burn energy hunting before you have shelter, water and signals.

## Critical steps

1. **Go to the water first.** Fish, crayfish and river snails are the easiest, safest protein in the wild — far better return than hunting mammals.
2. **Improvise hooks.** Bent pins, strong thorns, wire, sharpened can tabs, bone carved into a "V". Line: inner strands of paracord, dental floss, braided cloth threads.
3. **Find free bait.** Worms under damp rocks/logs, insects, grubs. The scraps of the first fish are bait for the second.
4. **Build a bottle trap for small fish.** Cut the top third off a PET bottle, invert it into the body (funnel inward), bait inside, sink it with stones. Fish swim in and can't find the way out.
5. **Rock-pool trap in streams.** A semicircular stone wall against the bank with a single gap; herd fish inside and close it with a rock.
6. **Let traps work and check them every few hours.** Traps fish while you build shelter or make signals — that is their advantage.
7. **Cook EVERYTHING thoroughly.** Fish and snails ALWAYS over fire until opaque/firm: freshwater carries real parasites.

## What is safe to eat (and what is not)

| ❌ Dangerous | ✅ Safe (cooked) |
|--------------|------------------|
| Wild mushrooms — NEVER (deadly ones mimic edible ones) | Freshwater fish, well cooked |
| Plants you can't identify 100% | Crayfish and river snails, cooked |
| The "universal edibility test" — a dangerous myth | Worms and insects, cooked (no bright colors) |
| Dead floating fish or stagnant-water fish | Live fish you caught yourself |
| Amphibians (colorful frogs/toads) | Grasshoppers/crickets cooked, spiny legs removed |
| Unknown berries "because birds eat them" | Only fruits you recognize with total certainty |

## Golden rules

- **If you don't recognize it with total certainty, do NOT eat it.** Hunger is uncomfortable; poisoning in the wild is deadly.
- **Cooked > raw, always.** Fire neutralizes most parasites and bacteria.
- Eat small portions of any new food first and wait a few hours.
- Water still comes first: digesting protein USES water — no water, no protein meals.

## Minimal gorge hook

1. Carve a 2-3 cm splinter of bone/hard wood, sharpened at both ends.
2. Tie it by the MIDDLE to your line.
3. Hide it lengthwise inside the bait.
4. The fish swallows, the splinter turns crosswise — it works with no hook bend at all.

## Example: Emergency Food

**Situation:** Third day lost by a stream; shelter and water are handled, hunger bites.

**Do:** 1. Bottle trap baited with worms, sunk with stones. 2. Semicircular rock wall in the pool. 3. Check every few hours while you work on signals. 4. Cook EVERYTHING until opaque and firm.

**Avoid:** Don't eat mushrooms or unknown plants, don't eat raw fish, don't burn the day hunting: traps work alone.

**Escalate:** If vomiting or diarrhea follows a meal, switch to the poisoning guide and prioritize rehydration; if hunger clouds judgment, stay by the water and your signals.
