# Moving Through Swamp and Mangrove

In the swamp you do not walk: **you negotiate every step**. Mud exhausts you 5 times more than a trail, and the straight line is the slowest route.

## Critical steps

1. **Probing pole ALWAYS.** A strong 2 m branch: every step is tested before you load it. What looks green and firm can be a crust over deep mud.
2. **Step on roots and clumps, not the clearing.** Mangrove roots and grass hummocks hold; the "clear path" between them is the mud pit. Route from clump to clump even if it zigzags.
3. **Trapped in deep mud: LIE DOWN.** Fighting upright buries you deeper. Spread out: chest on the pole or backpack, and "swim" slowly backward pulling out one leg at a time. Slow wins; panic buries.
4. **Tide in the mangrove = your clock.** Enter on a FALLING tide, leave before it rises: the mangrove at high tide erases the ground and the references. Set your deadline hour when you enter.
5. **Fixed bearing or eternal circle.** Without references, the swamp makes you walk in circles: set a bearing by compass/sun and confirm it every 50 steps with a distant object (tall tree, antenna).
6. **Waist-deep water: drag, do not lift.** Sliding your feet along the bottom avoids holes and the exhaustion of "pulling" each boot out of the mud. Backpack hip belt loose in case you must drop it.
7. **Get out of the water BEFORE nightfall.** The night swamp belongs to reptiles and mosquitoes: dry platform, smoky fire against insects and closed-up clothing (hazards guide).

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|----------|------------|
| Crossing the "more direct" mud clearing | Root to root, clump to clump |
| Fighting upright against mud that swallows | Lie down, spread weight, swim backward |
| Entering the mangrove on a rising tide | Enter as it falls and leave with margin |
| Walking "straight" without confirming the bearing | Compass/sun + distant reference every 50 steps |
| Lifting each foot out of the mud | Drag your feet along the bottom |
| Staying in the water at nightfall | Dry platform + fire before dark |

## Pace and energy

- Rest BEFORE you are exhausted: 10 min per hour on dry ground; the mud charges interest.
- Hydrate twice what you think you need: you sweat more than you notice in the humidity.
- Check legs and feet at every rest: leeches and wounds the water hides (remove leeches by sliding a fingernail along their mouth, without tearing or burning).

## Example: Moving Through the Swamp

**Situation:** Shortcut through the mangrove toward the coast; after 40 minutes the mud goes from ankle to knee and the tide starts to rise.

**Do:** 1. Check your deadline hour: the tide now rules — ABORT the shortcut. 2. Turn around stepping exactly in your own footprints and on known clumps. 3. Pole ahead on every step. 4. If the mud swallows you: lie down, weight on the chest over the pole, leg by leg. 5. On dry ground: check legs, hydrate and go around the mangrove on firm land.

**Avoid:** Do not "hurry" by crossing direct clearings, do not continue while the tide rises, do not drop the pole, do not stay upright fighting.

**Escalate:** If someone is trapped and cannot get out with the technique: do NOT enter the same mud — pole/rope/knotted shirt from a firm base, and weight spread with branches under their chest; trigger the mesh SOS with your exact position.
