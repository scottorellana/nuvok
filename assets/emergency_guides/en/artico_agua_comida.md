# Water and Food in Extreme Cold

Surrounded by frozen water you can die dehydrated: **eating snow chills your core** and the dry cold dehydrates you without sweating. And the cold charges double the calories.

## Critical steps

1. **NEVER eat snow directly.** Melting it with body heat costs the heat that keeps you alive and burns twice the water in energy. Only minimal amounts if you are warm and moving.
2. **Melt, do not suck:** fire/stove with a pot (start with a splash of liquid water in the bottom so the pot does not scorch), or a dark bag of snow in the sun / a bottle inside the coat between layers (not against the skin).
3. **ICE > snow for melting:** ice gives twice the water per volume with half the fuel. Old bluish freshwater ice; YOUNG sea ice is salty (old bluish ice loses its salt).
4. **Drink WARM and often.** In the cold you do not feel thirst but you exhale liters: clear urine is your gauge. Warm water does not spend your heat warming it up inside you.
5. **Eat FAT and double calories:** the cold burns 4,000-6,000 kcal/day. Fat is queen (more than double the energy per gram); a hot meal before sleeping is your night heating.
6. **Protect water from freezing:** bottle upside DOWN (ice forms at the top: the thread does not seal shut), inside the coat by day and in the sleeping bag/shelter at night.
7. **Ice fishing as a food plan:** the water under the ice has fish all winter — hole + baited line (fishing guide); check the ice thickness ALWAYS (hazards guide).

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|----------|------------|
| Eating fistfuls of snow out of thirst | Melt and warm before drinking |
| Melting snow in a dry pot over a live flame | A splash of water first or the pot scorches |
| Young sea ice "because it is ice" | Only old bluish or freshwater ice |
| Rationing water "because melting is costly" | Drink until urine is clear: cold dehydration is treacherous |
| A "light" lean diet in extreme cold | Fat and double rations: the cold is paid in calories |
| Bottle upright out in the open | Upside down, inside the coat/bag |

## The overnight snow oven

- Fill the bottle/bag with snow BEFORE sleeping and put it in the sleeping bag (not against skin): you wake up with water without spending fuel.
- A thermos or a bottle wrapped in a sock = liquid water until morning.

## Example: Water and Food in the Cold

**Situation:** Second day of waiting in the snow shelter; half a stove tank left, little liquid water and crackers + a tin of peanut butter.

**Do:** 1. Go out for bluish ICE (not snow) with the guide rope. 2. Melt in batches starting with your liquid water; refill bottles and one goes upside down into the sleeping bag. 3. Hot ration before sleeping: the peanut butter is your best weapon (fat). 4. Drink warm every hour until urine is clear. 5. Fuel at half: prioritize melting over cooking (the crackers need no fire).

**Avoid:** Do not suck snow "in the meantime", do not melt with a dry pot, do not skip the night meal to ration, do not leave the bottles outside.

**Escalate:** If the fuel runs out: dark bag with snow in the midday sun and a bottle between coat layers on the move; if someone stops urinating or gets confused, treat it as an emergency (hypothermia/dehydration) and prioritize all the warm water for them.
