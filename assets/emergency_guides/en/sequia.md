# Drought and Water Scarcity

Drought is the slow catastrophe: **it has no siren**, and when the well drops it is already too late to start. You survive by managing BEFORE the crisis.

## Critical steps

1. **Start rationing BEFORE it runs out.** At the first drought warning: water inventory (tanks, cisterns, bottles), measured consumption per person, and weekly targets. The fatal mistake is living "normally" until the day without water.
2. **Inviolable hierarchy:** 1) drinking (3 L/person/day), 2) cooking, 3) hand hygiene, 4) everything else. Drinking water is NEVER spent on floors or irrigation.
3. **Reuse in a cascade:** washing vegetables → that water washes dishes → that one irrigates or flushes the toilet. Bucket shower (5 L well used) and a bucket under the shower while it warms up.
4. **Harvest every drop from the roof:** gutters to drums/cisterns (discard the first minutes of rain); a 50 m² roof gives 50 L for every millimeter of rainfall. Cover EVERY container (evaporation + mosquitoes).
5. **Protect the shared source:** communal well/cistern with a schedule, a measured share per family and a guarded key — community organization prevents the conflict that thirst ignites (organization guide).
6. **Doubtful water counts, with treatment:** old wells, murky cisterns: settle + filter + chlorine/boil (water guides). In a drought, a treated "bad" source is better than none.
7. **Watch the fragile and the heat:** the elderly and children dehydrate quietly; combined with a heat wave = heat stroke guide. Dark urine in someone = their ration goes up TODAY.

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|----------|------------|
| Living "normally while there is some" | Ration from the first warning |
| Irrigating/washing the car with potable water in a drought | Reuse cascade; potable only for the vital |
| Uncovered containers "so they air out" | Covered: evaporation and dengue |
| Each family fighting for the well on its own | Schedule, measured share and communal custody |
| Discarding the cistern's murky water | Treat it: settle+filter+disinfect |
| Waiting for official rationing to act | Your own inventory and targets NOW |

## Numbers that save

- Vital minimum: 3 L/person/day drinking+cooking; 15 L with basic hygiene.
- A 200 L drum = a family of 4 for ~2 weeks at the vital minimum.
- 1 mm of rain × 1 m² of roof = 1 liter harvested.

## Example: Drought

**Situation:** Third month without rain; municipal water arrives for 2 hours every 3 days and your cistern is at half.

**Do:** 1. Inventory today: total liters ÷ person ÷ day = your real deadline. 2. Targets: 15 L/person/day maximum, mandatory reuse cascade. 3. The 2 hours of municipal water: fill EVERYTHING (cistern, drums, tub) in a rehearsed order. 4. Gutters ready in case it rains. 5. Neighborhood meeting: communal well with a schedule and measure, and the mesh announces when the water arrives.

**Avoid:** Do not spend it on irrigation/the car, do not leave drums uncovered, do not trust "it comes tomorrow", do not leave the well conflict without rules.

**Escalate:** If the communal source drops to critical: prioritize drinking over ALL hygiene except hands, activate collection/treatment of doubtful sources (water guides), and organize collective hauling from the nearest live source — as a group, in shifts and in the early morning (the heat).
