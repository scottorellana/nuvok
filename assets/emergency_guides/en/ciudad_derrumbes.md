# Collapses and Collapsed Buildings

After the collapse, **the greatest danger is the second collapse** — and badly done spontaneous rescue kills rescuers. Order and hearing save more than strength.

## Critical steps

1. **If you are trapped: protect your air.** Cover nose and mouth with cloth (dust suffocates and blinds), light no flame (gas), and do NOT shout at first: tap 3 times on a pipe or slab, in rounds, and listen. The voice wears out; metal carries.
2. **Do not move blindly.** Feel before rolling over: moving can destabilize your survival pocket. If there is light/space, move toward it calmly, testing every support.
3. **From outside: STOP and listen.** Organized total silence (engines off) every 10-15 min: 3 taps = someone alive. Mark where it was heard.
4. **Do not climb the rubble pile.** Your weight collapses survival pockets underneath. Work from the edges, removing piece by piece in a human chain, never pulling what bears weight.
5. **Aftershocks: everyone out and away.** After the first collapse, what is damaged keeps falling. Work zone with an agreed escape route and a lookout watching the structure.
6. **Mark what has been searched.** Visible sign (paint, cloth) on every searched zone with date/time: duplicated effort costs lives elsewhere.
7. **Water and voice to the trapped person.** If there is contact: pass water if possible, speak to them by name, tell them the rescue is advancing — morale sustains critical hours. Limbs crushed >15 min: do NOT free them without medical preparation (crush syndrome) — request medical instructions over the mesh.

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|----------|------------|
| Running over the rubble shouting names | Edges, organized silence, listening for taps |
| Pulling the beam "that is in the way" | Nothing that bears weight moves without shoring |
| Striking a match/lighter to see | Flashlight only: accumulated gas explodes |
| Suddenly freeing a limb crushed for hours | Medical preparation first (crush syndrome) |
| Going back in for documents/pets | What is damaged keeps falling with the aftershocks |
| Shouting nonstop if you are trapped | 3 taps on metal, regular rounds, save your air |

## Survival pocket kit (if you live in a seismic zone)

- Under the bed: closed shoes, flashlight, whistle, bottle of water.
- A whistle under rubble is worth a thousand shouts.
- Agreed as a family: meeting point outside (the mesh meeting point guide).

## Example: Collapsed Building

**Situation:** After the earthquake, the neighboring house partially collapsed; someone can be heard and neighbors arrive wanting to help.

**Do:** 1. Organize: engines and radios off, total silence, 3 answering taps — locate the point. 2. Aftershock lookout + escape route for everyone. 3. Human chain from the edge removing piece by piece. 4. Contact: water, name, encouragement; request medical guidance over the mesh if there is crushing. 5. Mark searched zones and rotate out tired people.

**Avoid:** Do not climb the pile, do not move loaded columns/beams, do not light flames, do not work without a lookout.

**Escalate:** If it smells of gas or a pipe hisses: evacuate the perimeter, nothing electrical and no flames, shut the main valve if safe (urban hazards guide); with strong aftershocks, EVERYONE out until re-assessed.
