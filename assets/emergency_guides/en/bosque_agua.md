# Water in the Forest

**Rule of 3s: you die in 3 days without water** — and dehydrated you think poorly from day one. In the forest water exists; you must know how to read it.

## Critical steps

1. **Listen before you search.** At dawn and dusk, stand still for 2 minutes: running water can be HEARD from far away. Walk toward the sound.
2. **Go down, not up.** Water obeys gravity: follow gullies and low ground downhill. Birds at dusk fly TOWARD water.
3. **Look for the green indicators.** Greener, denser vegetation, reeds, willows, thick moss = water near the surface.
4. **Rain = free, safe water.** Stretch plastic/tarp in a V into a container. It is the ONLY water you can drink untreated.
5. **Transpiration trap.** A plastic bag tied tightly over a leafy branch in the sun: in 4-6 hours it collects clean water from the leaves (little, but safe).
6. **Filter cloudy water BEFORE purifying.** Layered cloth/t-shirt: removes mud and visible bugs. Filtering does NOT purify.
7. **ALWAYS purify found water.** Boil 1 minute at a rolling boil (3 min at altitude) or use chlorine (2 drops/liter of 5% bleach, wait 30 min — calculator in Tools).

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|-----------|-----------|
| Drinking from puddles or still water "because it looks clear" | Running water only, ALWAYS purified |
| Eating snow or drinking untreated out of desperation | Diarrhea dehydrates MORE: always purify |
| Waiting until you're thirsty to ration | Drink regular sips; thirst arrives late |
| Walking in daytime heat searching for water | Search early or at dusk; you sweat less |
| Drinking urine or seawater | They accelerate dehydration — never |
| Trusting unknown fruits/vines for their liquid | Only if you recognize them with total certainty |

## Sources ranked by safety

1. **Directly collected rain** — safe untreated.
2. **Leaf transpiration (bag)** — safe, slow.
3. **Springs and fast creeks** — always purify.
4. **Rivers and pools** — filter + purify.
5. **Still water** — last resort: filter + hard boil.

## How much water you need

- Resting in shade: **2-3 liters/day**.
- Walking with a load: **4-6 liters/day**.
- Rationing water does NOT work: ration your SWEAT (move early/late, rest in shade).

## Example: Water in the Forest

**Situation:** Second day lost in the forest, your bottle has been empty since last night and the sun is strong.

**Do:** 1. Stay in the shade until mid-afternoon. 2. Listen in silence for 2 minutes: locate running water. 3. Head downhill along the gully following the densest green. 4. Filter with your shirt and boil 1 minute or chlorinate 30 min. 5. Rig the tarp in a V in case it rains.

**Avoid:** Don't walk at midday, don't drink unpurified, don't eat without water (digestion uses water), don't climb hills looking for water.

**Escalate:** If diarrhea or vomiting follows doubtful water, switch to the poisoning guide and prepare oral rehydration (calculator); after 24 h without finding water, prioritize rescue signals over walking on.
