# Hurricane

## Before the hurricane
1. **Monitor alerts** — Listen to radio/TV and official notifications
2. **Reinforce windows** — With plywood or storm shutters
3. **Trim trees** — Remove dead branches that could fall
4. **Clean drains** — Ensure they drain properly
5. **Fill gas tank** — Fuel will be scarce afterward

## During the hurricane
1. **Stay indoors** — Don't go outside even during the eye
2. **Stay away from windows** — Use interior windowless room
3. **Close all doors** — Reduces pressure differential
4. **If roof fails** — Cover yourself with mattresses or blankets
5. **DO NOT go outside** — Even if it seems calm, it may be the eye

## After the hurricane
1. **Wait for "all clear"** — Downed power lines/poles may exist
2. **Use flashlight** — Don't use candles (gas leaks possible)
3. **Avoid flooded roads** — Don't know what's underneath
4. **Document damage** — Take photos for insurance
5. **Boil water** — Until confirmed safe

## Saffir-Simpson Scale
- **Cat 1** (74-95 mph): Minimal damage
- **Cat 2** (96-110 mph): Extensive damage
- **Cat 3** (111-129 mph): Devastating
- **Cat 4** (130-156 mph): Catastrophic
- **Cat 5** (157+ mph): Complete destruction
