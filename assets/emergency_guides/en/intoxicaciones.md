# Poisoning

> ⚠️ This guide does not replace professional medical care. Get medical help as soon as possible.

## What NOT to do (applies to almost everything)

- ❌ **DON'T induce vomiting** (what burned going down burns coming up; and it can enter the lungs). Only a poison center can order it.
- ❌ NO milk, oil, lemon, coffee or home "neutralizers".
- ❌ NOTHING by mouth if drowsy or seizing.
- ❌ DON'T rush into a room full of gas/smoke to rescue someone without ventilating first: two victims are worse than one.

## SWALLOWED something (medicines, bleach, pesticide, fuel)

1. **Sweep out anything left in the mouth** with a cloth-wrapped finger.
2. **Identify WHAT, HOW MUCH and HOW LONG AGO.** Keep the container/label — it's gold for the doctor.
3. Corrosive (bleach, lye, acids) and AWAKE: they may rinse the mouth and spit. Small sips of water ONLY if the product label says so.
4. **Transport with the container.** Fast if: a child, a pesticide/strong medicine, or any symptoms.
5. Drowsy or unconscious: **recovery position on the side**, nothing by mouth, watch the breathing.

## INHALED gas or smoke (carbon monoxide, wood smoke, exhaust, mixed cleaners)

Carbon monoxide (embers, engines, heaters in a closed room) has NO smell: suspect it when several people in the same house get headache, nausea, confusion or "flu" at the same time, or someone is found asleep next to a brazier/engine.

1. **Ventilate:** open doors/windows from outside if you can; kill the source ONLY if it doesn't risk you.
2. **Get them to FRESH AIR now** (hold your breath going in, stay low in smoke — clean air is near the floor).
3. Loosen clothing; slow deep breaths.
4. Unconscious → on their side; not breathing → CPR.
5. **Always see a doctor** after carbon monoxide even if "it passes": internal damage continues. Pregnant women: urgent, no exceptions.

## CHEMICAL on skin or in the eye

1. **Skin:** remove contaminated clothing (with gloves) and **rinse under running water 20 minutes**. Dry powder: brush off BEFORE wetting.
2. **Eye:** rinse NOW with clean lukewarm water **15-20 minutes** non-stop, from the nose corner outward (don't wash into the good eye), holding the lid open. Remove contact lenses. It feels eternal: DON'T stop early — every minute of rinsing saves eyesight.
3. After rinsing: to a doctor (with the product label). Chemical in the eye = ALWAYS an emergency.

## When to get help / transport

- Every child who swallowed something questionable, even if they look fine.
- Any pesticide, fuel, corrosive, high-dose medicine or drug.
- Symptoms: vomiting, drowsiness, confusion, trouble breathing, burns in the mouth.
- Monoxide/smoke in a closed space: everyone exposed.

## Signs of deterioration

- Advancing drowsiness, slow or noisy breathing → on their side, be ready for CPR, transport fast.
- Seizure → guide "Seizures", nothing in the mouth.
