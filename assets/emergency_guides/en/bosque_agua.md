# Water in the Forest

**Rule of 3: you die in 3 days without water** — and when dehydrated, you think poorly from the first day. In the forest, water exists; you have to know how to read it.

## Critical steps

1. **Listen before searching.** At dawn and dusk, stay still for 2 minutes: running water can be HEARD from far away. Walk toward the sound.
2. **Go down, not up.** Water obeys gravity: follow gullies and low areas downward. Birds at dusk fly TOWARD water.
3. **Look for the green indicators.** Greener, denser vegetation, reeds, willows, abundant moss = water near the surface.
4. **Rain = free and safe water.** Stretch plastic/tarp in a V toward a container. It is the ONLY water you can drink without treating.
5. **Transpiration trap.** A plastic bag tied tightly over a leafy branch in the sun: in 4-6 hours it collects clean water from the leaves (little, but safe).
6. **Filter turbid water BEFORE purifying.** Cloth/T-shirt in layers: removes mud and visible bugs. Filtering does NOT purify.
7. **ALWAYS purify found water.** Boil 1 minute at a rolling boil (3 min in the mountains) or use chlorine (2 drops/liter of 5% bleach, wait 30 min — calculator in Tools).

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|----------|------------|
| Drinking from puddles or stagnant water "because it looks clear" | Only running water, and ALWAYS purified |
| Eating snow or drinking without purifying out of desperation | Diarrhea dehydrates MORE: always purify |
| Waiting until you are thirsty to ration | Drink regular sips; thirst arrives late |
| Walking during the day in heat looking for water | Search early or at dusk; sweat less |
| Drinking urine or seawater | They accelerate dehydration — never |
| Trusting unknown fruits/vines for their liquid | Only if you recognize them with total certainty |

## Sources ordered by safety

1. **Directly collected rain** — safe without treating.
2. **Leaf transpiration (bag)** — safe, slow.
3. **Springs and fast streams** — always purify.
4. **Rivers and pools** — filter + purify.
5. **Stagnant water** — last resort: filter + boil hard.

## How much water you need

- Resting in the shade: **2-3 liters/day**.
- Walking with a load: **4-6 liters/day**.
- Rationing water does NOT work: ration SWEAT (move early/late, rest in the shade).

## Example: Water in the Forest

**Situation:** Second day lost in the forest, the bottle has been empty since last night and the sun is strong.

**Do:** 1. Stay in the shade until mid-afternoon. 2. Listen for 2 minutes in silence: locate running water. 3. Go down through the gully following the densest green vegetation. 4. Filter with the T-shirt and boil 1 minute or chlorinate 30 min. 5. Set up the tarp in a V in case it rains.

**Avoid:** Do not walk at midday, do not drink without purifying, do not eat without water (digestion uses water), do not climb hills looking for water.

**Escalate:** If diarrhea or vomiting appears after drinking questionable water, go to the poisoning guide and prepare oral rehydration solution (calculator); if you have gone 24 h without finding water, prioritize rescue signals over continuing to walk.
