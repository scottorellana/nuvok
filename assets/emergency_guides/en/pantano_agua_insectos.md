# Water and Insects in the Swamp

Swamp paradox: **surrounded by water impossible to drink and by insects that kill more than the crocodiles** — dengue and malaria are the real danger of the week.

## Critical steps

1. **Never drink the still, dark water** of the swamp without the full ladder: settle → filter (cloth+charcoal+sand) → BOIL (better than chlorine here: organic matter eats the chlorine).
2. **Look for water that moves or falls:** inflowing tributaries, rain (the swamp's best source — capture it in every downpour), and HIGH bromeliads/tree hollows (filter and boil all the same).
3. **Swarm = smoke.** Constant fire with green leaves on top: smoke is your perpetual repellent. Camp with the smoke crossing the sleeping platform.
4. **Skin covered at dawn and dusk** (mosquito peak hours): long sleeves, pants inside socks, neck and head covered. Mud smeared on exposed skin works as an emergency barrier.
5. **Sleep HIGH and protected:** platform above the water/mud + any cloth as a mosquito net (a shirt over the face is worth more than nothing). The swamp floor at night belongs to the snakes.
6. **Drain your camp of breeding sites:** any container/hollow with still water within 20 m breeds mosquitoes in hours — empty or cover them.
7. **Fever in the swamp = alarm.** Fever + body aches days after bites: treat as dengue/malaria until proven otherwise — aggressive hydration (rehydration mix from the calculator), NO ibuprofen/aspirin if you suspect dengue (bleeding): paracetamol only, and evacuate.

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|----------|------------|
| Drinking dark water "filtered through a shirt" | Settle + filter + BOIL always |
| Chlorinating water with leaves/matter floating in it | Organic matter cancels the chlorine: boil |
| Sleeping on the ground "just one night" | High platform + smoke + cloth over the face |
| Scratching bites until they bleed | Mud/local cold; broken skin infects in hours |
| Ibuprofen for a fever suspected to be dengue | Paracetamol ONLY + rehydration + evacuation |
| Leaving cans/hollows with water by the camp | Empty or cover breeding sites within 20 m |

## Emergency repellents

- Smoke from a fire with green leaves (the king).
- Dry mud in a thin layer on exposed skin.
- Sleep with the wind in your favor (mosquitoes hunt against the wind).

## Example: Water and Insects

**Situation:** Second day stranded in mangrove country; the bottled water ran out and the mosquitoes ate the group alive last night.

**Do:** 1. Tarp ready for the afternoon downpour (source #1). 2. Meanwhile: water from the inflowing tributary, settled, filtered and boiled. 3. Raised sleeping platform + permanent smoky fire with green leaves. 4. Everyone covered at peak hours, mud on hands and neck. 5. Empty every can and hollow with water within 20 m.

**Avoid:** Do not drink from the still water surface, do not sleep at ground level, do not scratch until you bleed, do not let the fire die at dawn.

**Escalate:** High fever + pain behind the eyes in anyone in the group: suspect dengue — paracetamol (NEVER ibuprofen/aspirin), continuous oral rehydration, shade, and priority evacuation by triggering the mesh SOS.
