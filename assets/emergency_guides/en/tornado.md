# Tornado

Against a tornado you win neither in a car nor under a bridge: **you win underground or in the innermost room**, with minutes of head start.

## Critical steps

1. **Signs of an imminent tornado:** greenish sky, sudden large hail, strange silence followed by a roar like a freight train, rotating wall cloud or funnel. Any one of them = shelter NOW.
2. **The best shelter is underground:** basement or pit, under a strong table, away from windows. No basement: an INTERIOR room on the GROUND floor (internal bathroom, closet, hallway) — the more walls between you and the outside, the better.
3. **Protection position:** crouched, head against the internal wall, hands and arms covering head and neck; mattress/blankets/helmet on top. Shoes on (afterward there will be glass and nails everywhere).
4. **Mobile home/sheet-metal house = ALWAYS leave:** no trailer or light house withstands it — go to a solid neighboring shelter or, if there is none, a LOW ditch/depression far from trees and cars, face down, hands on the back of the neck.
5. **In a vehicle with the tornado visible far away:** drive at a RIGHT ANGLE to its path toward solid shelter. Close by or traffic stopped: abandon the car for the low ditch — NEVER under a bridge (a wind tunnel that accelerates debris).
6. **Silence is not the end:** the eye or a second vortex may follow. Leave only with the sky clearly open or the official all-clear, and leave carefully: gas, cables and loose structures (urban hazards guide).
7. **Afterward: step as if in a minefield.** Glass, nails, live cables, unstable debris — the collapse guide to search for people, and the mesh to report damage and reunite the family.

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|----------|------------|
| Opening windows "to equalize pressure" | A myth: you lose seconds and gain flying glass |
| Sheltering under the bridge | Low open ditch, face down |
| Trying to outrun it by car in its direction | Right angle to its path, or the ditch |
| Staying in the mobile home "holding on" | ALWAYS leave for solid shelter or low ground |
| Filming the funnel from the window | Interior room: glass is shrapnel |
| Going out at the first silence | Wait for open sky or the all-clear: there may be a second vortex |

## Prepare your safe room

- Chosen in advance: interior, ground floor, no windows.
- Inside: water, flashlight, whistle, shoes, accessible mattress, this app offline.
- 1-minute family drill: everyone knows how to get there IN THE DARK.

## Example: Tornado

**Situation:** Sudden hail and a growing roar; you are at home with your family and there is no basement.

**Do:** 1. Everyone to the downstairs internal bathroom, NOW — nobody goes to look. 2. Children at the back, adults covering them, mattress on top, shoes on. 3. Heads down, hands on the back of the neck, whistle at hand. 4. Once the roar passes: wait for a clear signal before going out. 5. Careful exit: check gas/cables, report over the mesh and to the meeting point.

**Avoid:** Do not open windows, do not go out to secure the car, do not peek "for a second", do not go out at the first silence.

**Escalate:** If the house fails while you are inside: get under the strongest structure (bathtub, solid table) and protect your head; afterward apply the collapse guide for trapped people, tourniquet/bleeding control for serious wounds and the mesh SOS — the first 30 minutes are yours.
