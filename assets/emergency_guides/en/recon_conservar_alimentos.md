# Preserve Food without Refrigeration

Without a refrigerator, the race is against bacteria: **salt, smoke, sun, and sugar** are humanity’s refrigerators — and knowing when NOT to eat something is half the craft.

## Critical steps

1. **First eat what dies first:** fresh meat/fish (hours), dairy (1 day), cooked foods (1 hot day), and leave rice, beans, cans, and roots for later. The week’s menu is dictated by decomposition.
2. **Sun drying (fruits, herbs, tomato, chili):** THIN slices on clean mesh/sheet metal, direct sun with breeze, covered with mosquito netting, turning 2 times a day; they go under a roof every night. Ready when it breaks instead of bending (2-4 days).
3. **Dried meat (jerky):** strips of 1 cm WITHOUT fat, salted on all sides, hung in sun/breeze or over low smoke, until dark and rigid (2-3 days). Fat does NOT dry: it goes rancid — remove it all.
4. **Salting fish:** butterflied open, THICK layer of salt inside and outside, stacked with weight overnight, then sun dried. Salt is cheap; poisoning is not.
5. **Smoking = drying + flavor + protective crust:** COLD/low smoke (do not cook) from hardwoods (oak, fruitwoods, dry mangrove) for hours. NEVER resinous woods (pine) or toxic woods.
6. **Store like treasure:** everything WELL dried in covered containers with salt/ash at the bottom, elevated, dark, and ventilated; check weekly — one moldy piece contaminates the jar.
7. **The discard-without-appeal rule:** sour/ammonia odor, slime, mold on meat, swollen or bubbling can = THROW IT OUT. In reconstruction, group poisoning is the second catastrophe.

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Error | ✅ Correct |
|----------|------------|
| Dry thick pieces “so it goes farther” | Thin strips: thick pieces rot inside |
| Leave the fat on meat to be dried | No fat: it goes rancid and ruins the piece |
| Smoke with pine/painted wood | Only natural hardwoods |
| Taste “just a little” of what smells strange | Discard without appeal |
| Store while damp “almost dry” | Dry enough to BREAK, or back into the sun |
| Swollen cans “boiled just in case” | Botulism: throw them out unopened |

## Other poor-person refrigerators

- **Zeer pot:** clay pot inside another with damp sand between them + damp cloth on top: lowers ~10 °C for vegetables (extra days).
- **Underground:** roots (potato, cassava, carrot) in a box/hole with dry sand, cool and dark: weeks-months.
- **Honey/sugar and pickling in vinegar:** fruits and vegetables last months if the jar is clean and sealed.

## Example: Preserving Food

**Situation:** The power went out 2 days ago and a neighbor’s cow had an accident: there is 30 kg of meat and strong sun.

**Do:** 1. Today: divide tasks — debone, remove ALL the fat, strips of 1 cm. 2. Salt generously and hang on frames with mosquito netting, in sun and breeze. 3. Low oak fire downwind for the smoked batch. 4. Night: everything under a roof, covered. 5. Day 3: rigid pieces into jars with salt; the fat becomes rendered lard (immediate frying, do not store raw).

**Avoid:** Do not dry with fat, do not stack damp strips, do not leave the meat outdoors at night, do not store anything that bends instead of breaking.

**Escalate:** If someone vomits or has diarrhea after eating preserved food: oral rehydration solution (calculator) + poisoning guide, and the ENTIRE suspicious batch is thrown out and anyone who received it is notified by mesh — better to lose food than lose people.
