# Extreme First Aid

## Severe Bleeding

### Tourniquet (last resort)
1. Apply 5-7cm above wound
2. Tighten until bleeding stops
3. Write down time (max 2 hours)
4. DO NOT LOOSEN until hospital

### Direct pressure
1. Put clean cloth over wound
2. Press with both hands
3. Hold 10-15 minutes
4. DO NOT remove to check

### Gunshot wound
- DO NOT remove bullet
- Press around wound
- Immobilize area
- Seek help urgently

## Fractures

### Signs
- Severe pain
- Visible deformity
- Swelling
- Cannot move

### Immobilization
1. DO NOT move limb
2. Fix joint above and below
3. Use boards, branches, magazines
4. Immobilize as is

###注意事项
- DO NOT try to set in place
- Protect skin from splints
- Check distal circulation

## Burns

### Degrees
- 1st: Redness (sun)
- 2nd: Blisters (hot water)
- 3rd: White/black (fire)

### Treatment
1. Cool with water 20 min
2. DO NOT apply ice
3. DO NOT pop blisters
4. Cover with clean cloth
5. DO NOT apply toothpaste/butter

## CPR (Cardiac Arrest)

### Sequence
1. Check response
2. Call for help
3. 30 compressions + 2 breaths
4. Compress 5-6cm, 100-120/min

### In children
- One hand (less force)
- 30:2 same as adults

### Until
- Help arrives
- Person breathes
- You collapse from exhaustion

## Choking

### Conscious adult
1. 5 back blows
2. 5 abdominal thrusts (Heimlich)
3. Alternate until expelled

### Baby
1. 5 back blows (between shoulder blades)
2. 5 chest compressions (2 fingers)
3. Alternate

## Shock

### Signs
- Pale and cold skin
- Rapid and weak pulse
- Confusion
- Thirst

### Treatment
1. Lay flat on back
2. Elevate legs 30cm
3. Maintain temperature
4. DO NOT give fluids
5. Seek urgent help
