# Hypothermia and heat stroke

> ⚠️ This guide does not replace professional medical care. Get medical help as soon as possible.

---

## HYPOTHERMIA (losing more heat than the body makes)

No snow needed: rain + wind + exhaustion, or a river, is enough.

### When to suspect it

- **Mild:** intense shivering, clumsy hands, cold pale skin.
- **Severe:** shivering STOPS (very bad sign), slurred speech, confusion, drowsiness, stumbling, wanting "a little nap".

### What NOT to do

- ❌ NO rubbing or vigorous massage; NO direct hot water (abrupt rewarming can stop the heart).
- ❌ NO alcohol (feels warm, cools you further) and no cigarettes.
- ❌ DON'T handle a severe case roughly: a cold heart is very irritable.
- ❌ DON'T give them up for dead: "nobody is dead until warm and dead" — keep resuscitating during transport.

### Steps

1. **Out of cold/wind/wet.** Shelter, car, tent — whatever exists.
2. **Remove ALL wet clothing** (cut it off if needed) and dry them.
3. **Rewarm GRADUALLY:** layers of dry blankets under AND over; a hat (the head loses a lot); bottles of WARM (not hot) water wrapped in cloth at armpits, groin and chest — never on bare skin.
4. **Body heat:** get under the blankets with them if there's no other source.
5. **Awake and swallowing well:** WARM sweet drinks (cocoa, sugar water). No alcohol.
6. Severe (no shivering, confused): handle extremely gently, keep flat, transport urgently watching the breathing.

## HEAT STROKE (the body can no longer cool itself)

### When to suspect it

- Work/exercise in heat, or an elderly person/baby in a hot room.
- **Heat exhaustion (the doorway):** pouring sweat, weakness, nausea, cramps, dizziness — PALE, moist skin.
- **HEAT STROKE (emergency):** HOT and often dry skin (sweating stopped), confusion, aggression, stumbling, collapse, seizures. Extremely high temperature.

### What NOT to do

- ❌ NO "fever pills" (paracetamol/ibuprofen do NOT work here and harm liver/kidneys).
- ❌ NO alcohol or caffeine.
- ❌ DON'T leave them "resting in the sun".

## Steps

1. **Shade and cool NOW.** Strip extra clothing.
2. **COOL AGGRESSIVELY — this is what saves:**
   - Best: immerse to the neck in cool water (tub, stream) while supervising.
   - Otherwise: soak their clothes/body with water and FAN non-stop (evaporation cools); wrapped ice at armpits, groin, neck.
3. **Alert and awake:** sips of cool water or oral rehydration salts. **Confused:** NOTHING by mouth.
4. Seizing → guide "Seizures". Unconscious → on their side, watch breathing.
5. **Urgent transport as soon as cooling starts** (keep cooling ON THE WAY, don't stop it).

## When to get help / transport

- Hypothermia not improving in 30 min, or severe (no shivering/confused) → urgent.
- EVERY heat stroke with confusion is a hospital case even if they "recover" — organs can fail hours later.

## Signs of deterioration

- Stops responding or breathing → CPR.
- Vomits → always onto the side.
