# Survival Food

## Golden rule

**"If you do not know it, DO NOT eat it"**

- 90% wild plants are safe
- 10% remaining can kill you

## Insects (edible)

### Safe (cook first)
- Crickets, locusts
- Ants (especially leaves)
- Maguey worms
- Cicadas

### DO NOT eat
- Hairy caterpillars
- Brightly colored insects
- Bees/wasps with stingers
- Dead insects in decomposition

## Fish

### How to clean
1. Remove entrails immediately
2. Remove head and tail
3. Wash with clean water
4. Cook completely

### Toxic fish (do not eat)
- Pufferfish
- некоторые types of tropical fish

## Mammal meat

### Cleaning technique
1. Bleed completely
2. Remove skin and entrails
3. Discard organs except heart/liver
4. Cook well (no medium)

### Safe animals
- Small game: rabbit, hare, squirrel
- Rodents: rats, mice
- Deer (if present)

### Dangerous animals
- Animals dead from disease
- Carnivores (foxes, raccoons)
- Animals with foam at the mouth

## Safe plants

###注Fruits
- Blackberries, wild strawberries
- Wild apple (without seeds)
- Grapes, figs

### Vegetables
- Fern stems (boil 2x)
- Thistle roots
- Lily tubers

### DO NOT eat
- Plants with white latex
- Red or black berries in clusters
- Mushrooms (unless you are an expert)
- Plants with onion-like bulbs

## Purification

### Always cook
- All meat: 15+ minutes
- Fish: until flesh separates
- Insects: until they croak

### When there is no fire
- Fish: clean and eat raw if fresh
- Insects: safe raw (except larvae)
- Fruits: only the ones you know

## Preservation

### Without refrigeration
- Dry in the sun (meat + fish)
- Smoke if there is smoke
- Salt if there is salt

### Approximate shelf life
- Dried meat: 1 week
- Dried fish: 3-5 days
- Tubers: several weeks
- Dried fruits: 1 month

## Example: Survival Food

**Situation:** The power goes out for several days and you need to ration food for four people.

**Do:** 1. Stop for 30 seconds and define the main danger. 2. Gather water, shelter, light, communication, and a safe route. 3. Execute the simplest action that reduces risk now. 4. Mark what was done in notes/checklist so another person can continue.

**Avoid:** Do not use battery without a plan, do not cross moving water, do not eat unknown plants, do not separate from the group, and do not wait until dark to set up shelter.

**Escalate:** Change the plan if the weather worsens, body temperature drops, safe water is lacking, there are vulnerable children/older adults, or you lose orientation.

**Quick check:** before closing the guide, say out loud what happened, what you did, what is missing, and who remains responsible. This example is a guided practice; adapt the steps to your environment and prioritize professional help when it exists.
