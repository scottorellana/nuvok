# Swamp Hazards

The swamp's dangers are patient: **the caiman waits at the bank, the snake on the branch, and infection in every wet scratch**.

## Critical steps

1. **Respect the edge of open water.** Caimans/crocodiles hunt at the water-land boundary, above all at dawn/dusk. Take water and cross at WIDE, visible points, never among floating vegetation, and never in murky water at night.
2. **Escape distance: 5 meters from the water** when camping or cleaning fish (the smell draws them). Never swim "to cool off" in murky tropical water.
3. **Look at the branch before grabbing it.** Snakes rest ON branches at the height of your face and hand. In the swamp the pole parts the vegetation BEFORE your body passes.
4. **Caiman attack (very rare if you respect 1-3):** fight for the eyes and snout, noise and blows — they usually release what fights back at a cost. On land: run straight and away from the water (on land they do not chase far).
5. **Every wound gets washed and covered IMMEDIATELY.** Swamp water infects scratches within hours: potable/boiled water + soap, dry, cover. Check feet and legs 2 times a day — a blister here is a doorway.
6. **Leeches: fingernail, not fire.** Slide a fingernail/flat knife along its mouth to release it; burning it or tearing it off makes it vomit into the wound (infection). Wash and cover afterward.
7. **The small fauna's hooks:** river stingrays (shuffle your feet along the bottom), crabs and catfish with spines (do not put your hand in holes), fire ants (check where you stop to rest).

## What NOT to do

| ❌ Mistake | ✅ Correct |
|----------|------------|
| Camping/cleaning fish at the water's edge | 5+ m from the water, scraps far from camp |
| Swimming in murky water "a quick dip" | Never in the murky tropics: you do not see what sees you |
| Grabbing branches without looking | The pole first, the eyes second, the hand last |
| Tearing off/burning the leech | Fingernail along the mouth, wash and cover |
| Ignoring the "tiny" scratch | Wash+cover EVERYTHING; check 2×/day |
| Walking the bank at night | The night shift belongs to the reptiles |

## Infection signs that cannot wait

- Spreading redness, local heat, pus, red lines climbing from the scratch.
- Fever after a wet wound.
- Action: wash with boiled water+soap 2×/day, cover, and evacuate early — in the tropics infection runs.

## Example: Swamp Hazards

**Situation:** You fished at dusk by the channel; the fish must be cleaned and camp set up, and someone has leeches on their leg.

**Do:** 1. Camp 10 m from the water, high platform. 2. Fish get cleaned FAR away and the scraps go into moving water downstream, not next to camp. 3. Leeches off with a fingernail, wash with boiled water and cover. 4. Smoky fire, foot check for the whole group. 5. Nobody returns to the bank at night.

**Avoid:** Do not clean fish at the edge of dark water, do not burn the leeches, do not leave scraps next to where you sleep, do not send anyone alone for water at dusk.

**Escalate:** Snakebite: immobilize, note the exact time, and bites guide + mesh SOS; a wound with spreading redness or fever = priority evacuation — in the swamp, infection is the slow emergency that wins if you let it.
